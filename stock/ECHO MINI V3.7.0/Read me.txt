Notes:
1. Since the update is extensive, the firmware upgrade will first format the internal memory. It is recommended to back up your songs before upgrading.

3. When connected to a computer or smartphone via OTG, the ECHO MINI will automatically display a prompt to select between USB DAC mode or data transfer mode, and the selection will be remembered as default.
